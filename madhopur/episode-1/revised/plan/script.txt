Opening shot. Tight on young Meera as she looks up. Tilt just enough to catch a fireball crossing the sky. By the third second, glass is rattling in the shopfront behind her.

Across the road, Meera’s mother simply waves her hand. Her father stands beside her. Meera starts to wave back. An impact sends dust across the street. Keep the camera with Meera as she loses sight of them.

MEERA: मम्मी? … कहाँ हो?

Sound. Drop the market chatter at the impact. Bring back only Meera’s voice and the rattle of a loose shop shutter.

Villagers at street level look up as a blazing object that appears to be a meteor streaks overhead. It tears through faraway fields and crushes shops along a distant road, with dust and debris rising behind it.

From far away, one deep, heavy impact, as if something enormous has slammed into the ground. The low rumble rolls back through the village. Windows rattle, dust shakes loose, and the villagers stagger or brace themselves.

Devendra and Suman are driving home. Something streaks low across the road. Devendra hits the brakes. The pickup drops nose-first into a ditch.

Hold inside the cab. Suman lifts her head. A small boy peers at her through the cracked window. He looks as startled as she is.

Suman gets out, kneels and offers her hand.

SUMAN: डरो मत। तुम्हें चोट लगी है?

He takes her hand. Devendra climbs out behind her.

Devendra looks past the boy. A swathe of flattened maize ends at a massive dark craft towering over them and the trees. A small hatch stands open at its base, low enough for the child to have climbed out.

DEVENDRA: सुमन…

Final shots. Suman looks from the craft to the boy, who wraps both hands around hers. The boy, fully dressed, looks up: his eyes brighten to blue with a subtle glow and a faint marking of light pulses once through his kurta. A faint metallic tick comes from the cooling hull.
