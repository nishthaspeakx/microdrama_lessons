## Flagged for later

  - the craft sinks into the earth,
  - the authorities fence off the site,
  - the craft is made small again.
- **C3.** Episode 1's last picture is the boy's glowing eyes, not his hand. The opening match cut uses the hand-hold frame from scene 4 instead.
- **C6.** Yash's childhood scene was cut, so he needs a name card on his first shot.
- **C7.** Meera's green pendant needs a locked reference before the school-gate units.
- **C9.** The kitchen door must face the yard, gate and road, because Suman calls Veer from it while the bus passes.
