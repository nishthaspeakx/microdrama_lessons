# Madhopur, Episodes 1 and 2: inputs for Microdrama Studio

A clean start with just two episodes. Episode 1 is already made (`latest.mp4`); Episode 2 is the revised script.

## Step 1 · Upload idea

Upload these three files together:

1. `01-series-bible.md`: series, look, characters, locations, props and sound for both episodes
2. `02-episode-1-as-made.md`: the finished Episode 1, shot by shot, with dialogue and end state
3. `03-episode-2-script.md`: the Episode 2 shooting script with continuity notes

Do not upload this README.

Paste this into **Tell us about your series**, or write your own:

> मधोपुर का राज़ (The Secret of Madhopur). Two episodes for now. Episode 1 is already made and approved; treat its file as the reference for every face, costume, place and voice, and do not remake it. Produce Episode 2 from its script at 01:50, 9:16, in Hinglish with Devanagari subtitles. The craft is the half-buried dome from Episode 1; Episode 2 only mentions it. Veer's age at each point is as the script states.
