# मधोपुर का राज़ · The Secret of Madhopur · Series bible for Episodes 1 and 2

This bible covers only Episode 1, which is made, and Episode 2, which is to be produced. Looks are taken from the finished Episode 1 (`latest.mp4`); the times in brackets point to frames in that cut.

## The series

| | |
|---|---|
| Title | मधोपुर का राज़ (The Secret of Madhopur) |
| Episodes in scope | 2. Episode 1 · आसमान से क्या गिरा? (made, 01:27); Episode 2 · आज से ये हमारा बेटा है (to produce, 01:50) |
| Format | Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps |

| Setting | Madhopur, a fictional farming town in central India |
| Time | 2014, the day something falls from the sky; then 2026, twelve years later |
| Story so far | Something falls on Madhopur. Meera loses sight of her parents in the bazaar. Yash finds a teenage boy tied to a post in the maize. Suman and Devendra Sharma crash their pickup and meet a small boy, who leads them to a half-buried craft. |
| Episode 2 | That night the Sharmas decide the boy is their son and name him Veer. He grows up fast and strong. At seventeen, a green stone on Meera's chain weakens him, and on the way home Yash's car sends Veer off a river bridge. |

## Look

- **Light:** warm, low golden daylight with dust in the air, as in Episode 1. Skin tones look natural.
- **Kitchen:** warm practical light at night, with steel plates and lived-in textures.
- **The craft:** dark grey metal against warm earth.
- **Framing:** faces large and readable on a phone, with room at the top and bottom of the frame for app UI.
- **Camera:** still or gently moving for family scenes. Faster cutting for impacts and danger.

## Characters

Ages are given only as stated in the scripts.

### Veer Sharma (the boy)
- **Ages:** about five in 2014 (Episode 1 and the Episode 2 kitchen); then eight, eleven and thirteen in the montage; seventeen in 2026.
- **Episode 1 look:** short, soft black hair, dark eyes and a small slight build. He wears a cream kurta with a buttoned placket and long cuffs, and pale blue-grey pyjamas. See 00:53.6, 01:03.1 and 01:24.0.
- **Episode 2 kitchen:** the same cream kurta, under Devendra's checked shirt with the sleeves folded back twice.
- **Seventeen:** a school uniform with a bag on his shoulders.
- **Across ages:** keep the same face, eyes, brows and hairline.
- **Voice:** none in Episode 1. At seventeen he has his own voice, kept the same across episodes.
- **Abilities:** fast and strong from childhood. Meera's green stone weakens him while it is close.

### Suman Sharma
- **Role:** Devendra's wife. She takes the boy in and names him.
- **Look in 2014:** long black hair in one braid over the shoulder. She wears a sage-green patterned kurta with small white dots and a pale mint dupatta. See 00:38.5, 01:14.0 and 01:25.5.
- **2026:** subtly older, with the same face.
- **Voice:** her field voice from Episode 1, "डरो मत। तुम्हें चोट लगी है?", is the reference for the same evening.

### Devendra Sharma
- **Role:** Suman's husband, a farmer.
- **Look in 2014:** short black hair, a moustache and a broad build. He wears a dark red and navy checked shirt. See 00:47.4 and 01:16.3.
- **Episode 2 kitchen:** he is in his undershirt, because the boy is wearing his checked shirt.
- **2026:** subtly older.
- **Voice:** his field voice from Episode 1, "सुमन…", is the reference.

### Meera
- **2014:** a girl with two plaits tied with red ribbons. She wears a powder-blue frock with small white flowers, white leggings and red shoes. See 00:01.9 and 00:17.5.
- **2026:** Veer's schoolmate. She wears a green stone on a chain.
- **Voice:** a small, light girl's voice in 2014, "मम्मी? … पापा?". In 2026 her voice is not yet set.

### Meera's mother and father (2014 only)
- **Mother:** a maroon sari with a cream blouse. She waves with a paper bag. See 00:05.9.
- **Father:** a white kurta, grey trousers and a moustache.
- **What happened to them is not shown.**

### Yash
- **2014:** a boy in a grey T-shirt and dark shorts, with short black hair. See 00:23.0.
- **2026:** drives a sports car.

### The teenage boy at the post (2014)
- **Look:** an off-white shirt with a red S painted on it and dark trousers, tied to a wooden post in the maize. See 00:25.2.
- **Voice:** hoarse and urgent: "रुको! मेरी रस्सी खोल दो!"
- **His name is not given in these episodes.**

### Raghav (2026)
- **Role:** calls Meera over at the school gate. New in Episode 2.

## Sound and music

- **Dialogue:** record it first. Keep dialogue, ambience, effects and music on separate tracks.
- **Home theme:** felt piano, first heard when Devendra gives Veer the bigger half of the roti.
- **Montage:** a little guitar and a light drum pulse.
- **The green stone:** a low pressure sound, only while the stone is close.
- **The crash:** the score stops at contact. One splash, then muffled water.
