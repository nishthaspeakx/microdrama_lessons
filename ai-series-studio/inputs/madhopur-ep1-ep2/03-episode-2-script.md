## Scene 1 · 00:00–00:29 · INT. SHARMA FARMHOUSE, KITCHEN · NIGHT · 2014

A small hand picks a piece of गुड़ off a steel plate. One finger holds it still so it won't skid across the metal. A cream cuff peeks out under a much larger checked sleeve, folded back twice. In the warm light we recognise the boy from the field. He reaches across the table and puts the piece in Devendra's palm. Only then does he take one for himself.

Devendra leaves his hand open a moment. He's in his undershirt now; the checked shirt is his. He joins Suman at the sink, near enough to speak quietly.

**DEVENDRA**
सुबह लोग पूछेंगे, ये बच्चा कहाँ से आया।
*Subah log poochhenge, ye bachcha kahaan se aaya.*
English: In the morning people will ask where this child came from.

**SUMAN**
हम कहेंगे, हमें खेत में मिला।
*Hum kahenge, humein khet mein mila.*
English: We'll say we found him in the field.

**DEVENDRA**
और जो खेत में उसके पीछे था?
*Aur jo khet mein uske peechhe tha?*
English: And what was behind him in the field?

Suman turns. There is a third piece of गुड़ beside her plate. The boy is busy rolling his sleeve back up. Stay on her.

**SUMAN**
आज से ये हमारा बेटा है, देवेंद्र।
*Aaj se ye hamaara beta hai, Devendra.*
English: From today he is our son, Devendra.

Devendra sits down. He tears his roti and puts the larger half on the boy's plate. The first felt-piano notes enter here. He asks Suman the question with his eyes still on the boy.

**DEVENDRA**
वीर नाम कैसा रहेगा?
*Veer naam kaisa rahega?*
English: How about the name Veer?

**SUMAN**
वीर।
*Veer.*
English: Veer.

The boy looks up at her. Give them that beat.

**Sound:** let fingers on steel and the kitchen room tone carry the beginning. Keep Suman's decision clear of music.

## Scene 2 · 00:29–01:00 · INT./EXT. SHARMA FARM · FOUR AGES

**00:29–00:35 · Age eight, kitchen.** A steel tumbler tips. Suman reaches across the table. By the time her hand arrives, Veer has set it upright. A few drops run across her fingertips. He takes his next bite.

**00:35–00:42 · Age eleven, field.** The irrigation pump rattles. Devendra holds out his empty hand toward the barn for a spanner. The spanner touches his palm before he finishes calling. He looks past Veer at the barn across the long field. This time he checks whether anybody else saw.

**00:42–00:51 · Age thirteen, barn.** A bolt rolls under the tractor. Devendra crouches to reach it. The rear tyre lifts a few inches: his son is holding it up for him. Devendra pockets the bolt. Veer eases the wheel down.

**DEVENDRA**
वीर। स्कूल में ऐसा कुछ किया है?
*Veer. School mein aisa kuchh kiya hai?*
English: Veer. Have you done anything like this at school?

Veer shakes his head. In the doorway, Suman has heard the question.

**00:51–01:00 · Age seventeen, farm road, 2026.** The same doorway. Suman can see the yellow school bus beyond the gate.

**SUMAN**
वीर! Bus निकल गई!
*Veer! Bus nikal gayi!*
English: Veer! The bus has left!

**VEER**
मिलते हैं शाम को, माँ।
*Milte hain shaam ko, Maa.*
English: See you this evening, Maa.

Hold the empty farm gate as the dust lifts. Cut to the school gate. Veer smooths his hair. Behind him, the bus brakes.

**Edit and sound:**
- Cut cleanly between the four ages.
- Carry the steel ring, the pump rattle and the tyre settling across the cuts.
- A little guitar and a light drum pulse pick up with the run.

## Scene 3 · 01:00–01:22 · EXT. SCHOOL GATE · MORNING · 2026

Meera steps off the bus with an extra notebook. Frame the two faces close enough to see that Veer is pleased she caught up with him.

**MEERA**
आज भी bus छूट गई?
*Aaj bhi bus chhoot gayi?*
English: Missed the bus again today?

**VEER**
हाँ। पहुँच तो गया।
*Haan. Pahunch to gaya.*
English: Yes. I still made it.

She leans across to give him the notebook he left in class. The green stone on her chain swings forward. His fingers close on the cover, then falter. The notebook hits the ground between them.

**Sound:** pull the guitar away. A low pressure sound lasts just as long as she is close.

From the gate, Raghav calls Meera over. As she turns away, the stone goes with her. Veer bends, picks up the same notebook and tests his grip on its spine. Meera is already through the gate.

## Scene 4 · 01:22–01:50 · EXT. NARROW RIVER BRIDGE · LATE AFTERNOON · 2026

**01:22–01:32.** Walking home in the same uniform, bag on his shoulders, Veer stops at the railing. One wide shot places him, the bend and the river. Inside the approaching sports car, Yash sees a coil of wire lying in his lane.

**01:32–01:41.** The wire. Yash's eyes. The wheel turns. Tyres slip. Veer looks up into the headlights. Cut on impact to the railing giving way. One wide from the river side catches the car and the boy going over. The score stops at contact. One splash.

**01:41–01:50.** Underwater. The car sinks past Veer, catching his motionless face in its headlight beam. The bag strap is still on his shoulder. Hold until the audience has time to look for a breath. Muffled water. Cut to black.
